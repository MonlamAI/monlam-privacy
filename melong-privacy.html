1) Summary — the short version

Melong is an AI chat app. When you sign in and use chat, we process account details, messages, attachments, and voice input to provide the service. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising in the app.

  • Account data — name, email, and profile info when you sign in (for example with Google).
  • Chat & uploads — messages and files you send are processed on our servers to generate replies and save history.
  • No ads — we do not use your data for third-party advertising.
  • No data brokerage — we do not sell personal information to data brokers.

2) App permissions — what we access and why

The app may request access to device features so you can chat, attach media, and use voice:

  • Microphone — voice messages, dictation, and voice conversation. Audio is sent to our service for transcription and responses when you use those features.
  • Camera — take photos to attach to messages. Images are uploaded when you choose to send them.
  • Photos / media library — select images or files to attach. Content is uploaded only when you attach it to a message.
  • Network access — sign in, sync conversations, stream AI replies, and upload attachments.

You can revoke permissions in device settings; some features may stop working.

2a) Android permissions (typical declarations)

The Android app uses permissions only for the features above. Common declarations include:

  • INTERNET — connect to our API for sign-in, chat, and uploads.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — capture speech for dictation and voice chat.
  • CAMERA — capture photos for attachments.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (device-dependent) — pick photos and documents you choose to attach.

Exact permissions depend on your Android version and the features you use. They are not used for ads or cross-app tracking.

3) Data collection and processing

Account and profile

When you sign in (for example with Google Sign-In on Android), we receive and store:

  • Name or display name, email address, username, and user ID
  • Profile picture URL, if provided
  • Optional profile fields you provide (for example age range during onboarding)

Chat and usage

  • Messages you send and AI responses
  • Conversation titles, settings, and metadata (timestamps, model selection, pins)
  • Message feedback (like or dislike), if you use it

Chat content is processed on our servers. Depending on your message, we may search your uploaded documents or public web sources to answer you.

Files and audio

  • Documents and images (PDF, DOCX, TXT, JPG, PNG, WebP, and similar)
  • Audio you attach or record (MP3, WAV, M4A, WebM, and similar)

On your device

  • Sign-in tokens in secure storage
  • Language preference (for example English or Tibetan)

4) How we use information

  • Provide authentication, chat, attachments, voice transcription, and voice conversation
  • Sync conversations when you are signed in
  • Protect against abuse and secure the service
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Comply with law and improve reliability of the service

5) Third‑party services

We use infrastructure and service providers to host the app backend, store uploads, and run AI and speech processing. They access data only to perform services for us under confidentiality obligations.

Third-party AI processing. To generate a reply, we send your account email, messages, attached photos and files, and voice recordings to:

  • Monlam — models, speech-to-text, and speech synthesis.
  • Google (Gemini) — routing, embeddings, and ranking.
  • Tavily — web search, only when that turn uses the web.

This processing can happen outside India. The app asks for your permission before the first send. We do not sell this data, and agreeing does not allow us or those providers to train general models on your chats.

Google Sign-In (Android): Google processes sign-in under Google’s Privacy Policy. We receive limited profile information to create your Melong account.

Sign in with Apple (iOS, if available): Apple processes sign-in under Apple’s privacy terms; we receive limited account information to provide the service.

Chat replies may link to external websites; their privacy practices are their own.

6) Data retention & deletion

We retain account and chat data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Contact us to request deletion of your account and associated server data.

Sign-in tokens on your device are cleared when you sign out or uninstall the app. Local preferences are removed when you clear app storage or uninstall.

7) Children’s privacy

Melong is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

8) Your rights (EEA/UK, California, and similar)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. You can review account details in app settings, sign out, revoke permissions, or email us. We respond within a reasonable time as required by law.

Where applicable, we rely on contract, legitimate interests, consent (device permissions), and legal obligation.

9) Security

We use technical and organizational measures including HTTPS for network traffic and secure on-device storage for tokens. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.
